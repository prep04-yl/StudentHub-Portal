Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Click on any question below to expand or collapse the answer.

Q1. What is StudentHub Portal? +

StudentHub is a comprehensive web portal designed to provide students with single-window access to academic news, event registration, campus announcements, and administrative resources.

Q2. How can I register for events? +

You can browse upcoming events on the Events Page and click "View Details" or "Register" to sign up instantly using your student ID.

Q3. How do I contact student support? +

You can visit the Contact Page to submit an inquiry ticket or reach out directly to support@studenthub.edu.

Q4. How do I switch between Light and Dark mode? +

Click the 🌙 / ☀️ icon located at the top right of the page navigation header. Your preference will automatically be saved across your session!

Q5. What should I do if I forget my password? +

On the Login Page, click "Forgot Password?" to trigger password reset instructions sent to your registered student email address.
